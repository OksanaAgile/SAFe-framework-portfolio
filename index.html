The SAFe 6.0 Big Picture Framework

Empowering organizations to move quickly, improve innovation, align strategy to execution, and achieve sustainable Business Agility at scale.

1. Business Agility & Core Philosophy

Competitiveness and speed in the digital age

Business Agility is the ability to compete and thrive in the digital age by quickly responding to market changes and delivering innovative digital solutions.

Achieving the full benefits of Lean and Agile requires moving beyond traditional structures, establishing predictable cadences, aligning cross-functional teams, and eliminating heavy micro-management.

Key Objectives

  • Structured alignment across multiple Scrum teams
  • Seamless coordination across organizational layers
  • Continuous learning and faster response to change

2. The Foundation & Core Values

The belief and value system driving SAFe 6

1. Alignment

Everyone works toward the same strategic outcomes, connecting strategy directly to execution.

2. Transparency

Making all work, risks, and progress visible. Eliminates silos and builds deep organizational trust.

3. Respect for People

Valuing individuals, diversity, autonomy, and fostering relationships built on mutual trust.

4. Relentless Improvement

Continuously reflecting, problem-solving, and optimizing process and technical capability.

#2

4. Key Roles & Leadership in SAFe

Bridging executive strategy with team execution

SAFe Practice Consultants (SPCs)

Expert change agents and trusted advisors who guide the organization toward Business Agility:

  • Educator & Trainer: Trains leaders, POs, Scrum Masters, and teams.
  • Transformation Architect: Leads Value Stream Identification workshops & ART designs.
  • Coach & Facilitator: Mentors teams to optimize flow and collaboration.

Continuous Learning Culture

Built on three core pillars to foster psychological safety, technical growth, and innovation:

  • Learning Organization: Shared vision and collective mastery across squads.
  • Relentless Improvement: Constant reflection and root-cause analysis every sprint.
  • Culture of Innovation: Time and safety to experiment with new technologies and pipelines.
